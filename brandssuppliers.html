Why LuciHome Exists

A great catalog alone
has never been enough.

You can carry the best products in your category, and still lose the order to whichever supplier happened to be on the shortlist a purchasing manager already had. LuciHome exists because being sourced shouldn't depend on who a professional already knows — it should depend on what you actually make.

A real example

Imagine this: an architect in Bucharest is finalizing material specifications for a 40-unit residential project and needs a tile and stone supplier who can deliver at scale, on schedule. Today, they call three showrooms they already know, hoping one has what they need. On LuciHome, they browse verified suppliers by category, region, and availability directly inside the project workspace — and your catalog is simply there, ready to be specified.

FAQ

Questions suppliers
ask us most.

Quick answers before you register.

What type of catalogs can I list?

Any product catalog relevant to construction, renovation, or interior fit-out — furniture, lighting, tiles and stone, bathrooms, flooring, paints and coatings, doors and windows, kitchens, HVAC, smart home, and more. If professionals on LuciHome specify it, there's a place for it.

Can I list in more than one category?

Yes. Your supplier profile can span multiple categories — for example furniture and outdoor, or tiles and bathrooms — so your full range is visible in every relevant project search, not just one.

How are the buyers on LuciHome verified?

Architects, designers, developers, and property managers on the platform go through their own verification before they can send requests, so inquiries you receive come from real professionals working on real projects — not anonymous browsing.

Is LuciHome really free during the beta?

Yes. Every feature — your catalog, your profile, and your visibility to architects, designers, developers, and property managers — is completely free until the official launch on August 30, 2026.

What happens to my access after the official launch?

Free spots become limited to 250 per professional category after launch. Registering now as a Founding Member guarantees you one of those spots, plus 6 months of free access after launch.

Can I sell internationally through LuciHome?

Yes. LuciHome operates across 50+ countries, so architects, developers, and property managers investing outside your home market can discover and request your catalog directly.
